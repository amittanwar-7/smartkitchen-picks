Air Fryer Liner vs Foil vs Parchment Paper: What Is Safe and Useful?

By SmartKitchen Picks India · Last updated 2026-10-27

Cleaning an air fryer basket is the part many owners dislike, so liners are popular. But not every liner suits every recipe, and some can be a hazard if used incorrectly.

Here is how the three common options compare.

Silicone liners

  • Reusable and washable.
  • Good for marinated or sticky food.
  • Choose ones with holes for airflow, and a stated heat rating.
  • They add a small extra layer, so food may brown slightly less underneath.

Perforated parchment paper

  • Single use and easy to dispose of.
  • Pre-cut perforated sheets are designed for air fryers.
  • Always place food on top to hold it down, because a loose sheet can lift into the heating element.
  • Check the temperature rating on the pack.

Aluminium foil

  • Fine for small, secure pieces in some recipes, but never cover the whole basket, because that blocks airflow.
  • Avoid it with very acidic foods such as tomato or lemon marinades.
  • Do not let it touch the heating element.

Which should you pick?

For regular use, a silicone liner with holes is the simplest choice. Keep perforated parchment for messy recipes, and use foil sparingly. In every case, never preheat with a loose liner in the basket, and always read your air fryer's manual.

Frequently asked questions

Do liners change cooking time?

They can slightly, because they reduce airflow under the food. Check your food a little earlier the first few times.

Can I use normal baking paper?

Plain baking paper without perforations blocks airflow and can fly up. Use sheets designed for air fryers and weigh them down with food.

Are silicone liners safe?

Food-grade silicone with a stated heat rating is generally considered safe for normal use. Avoid unbranded items with no rating.
