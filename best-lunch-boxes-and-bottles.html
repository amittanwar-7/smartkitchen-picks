Choosing the right material

  • Stainless steel (look for 304 or food-grade on the listing) is durable and does not hold smells or stain. It cannot go in the microwave.
  • BPA-free plastic is light and good for children, and many designs are microwave-safe. Check the listing for a microwave-safe rating before heating.
  • Glass keeps flavours clean and is microwave-friendly, but it is heavy and breakable, so it suits office desks more than school bags.

Insulation and leak-proofing

Vacuum-insulated bottles and flasks use two walls with a vacuum between them. When you pour in hot water, the outside of a good one stays cool to the touch. Single-wall bottles are lighter but do not keep temperature.

For lunch boxes, a silicone gasket in the lid and locking clips are what stop leaks. Test any new box by filling it with water, closing it and turning it upside down over a sink before you pack curry in it.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Microwaving a steel container.

Frequently asked questions

How do I clean a steel water bottle?

Use warm water, dish soap and a bottle brush. For smells, soak overnight in water with a spoon of baking soda, then rinse and dry fully.

Can I microwave a plastic lunch box?

Only if the listing states it is microwave-safe. Even then, remove the lid or open the vent first.

How can I test if a flask is truly insulated?

Fill it with hot water and close it. After a few minutes, the outside should stay cool. If the outside gets hot, the insulation is not working.

What size lunch box should I pick?

Count the items you pack. Most adults need about two compartments for rice or roti and one for sabzi or dal. Children need smaller, lighter boxes.
