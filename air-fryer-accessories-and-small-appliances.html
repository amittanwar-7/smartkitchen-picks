Match accessories to your air fryer

  • Check your air fryer's capacity in litres and the inside diameter of the basket. Accessories are usually listed with a diameter in inches or centimetres.
  • Leave room for air to flow. If an accessory fills the basket from wall to wall, food will cook unevenly.
  • Check the heat rating. Anything you put in must be safe for at least 200°C. Look for 'heat resistant' with a stated temperature.

Air fryer safety rules

  • Never preheat with a loose liner or paper in the basket. The fan can lift it into the heating element. Always put food on top of the liner to hold it down.
  • Do not cover the basket completely with foil or a liner, which blocks airflow. Use liners with holes.
  • Keep the air fryer away from the wall and keep the vents clear.
  • Avoid aerosol cooking sprays, which can damage non-stick coatings. A pump sprayer filled with oil is a better choice.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying accessories before measuring the basket.
  • Preheating with a loose liner inside.
  • Overfilling the basket. Food needs space for hot air to circulate.
  • Using metal tools that scratch the non-stick coating.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need accessories for an air fryer?

No, an air fryer works without them. Liners, racks and moulds are optional extras that make cleaning easier and expand what you can cook.

Can I use aluminium foil in an air fryer?

A small piece under food is generally fine if it does not block the airflow. Never cover the entire basket, and avoid using it with very acidic foods.

What size accessories should I buy?

Match the basket's inside diameter. If a listing gives sizes in inches, measure your basket in inches or centimetres and leave a small gap.

How do I clean an air fryer basket?

Let it cool, then wash with warm soapy water and a soft sponge. Avoid steel wool, which damages the non-stick coating.
