If you own an air fryer, a few inexpensive accessories open up many more recipes. Always check the size of your fryer's basket first.

What to check before buying

  • Match accessory size to your fryer's capacity in litres.
  • Use only accessories marked heat safe up to at least 200°C.
  • Never block the air vents with liners or foil.
