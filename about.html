About SmartKitchen Picks India

This site publishes buying guides for budget kitchen products in India. Our goal is to explain what to check before you buy, such as materials, safety, size and warranty, so that you spend your money on the right thing.

How we write our guides

We research each product category, explain how the products work, and list what to look for and what to avoid. We do not test products ourselves, so we never claim hands-on testing and we do not rank products by performance. Where a statement depends on a product's specification, we tell you to check the listing.
