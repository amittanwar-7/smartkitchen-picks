Digital Kitchen Scale Buying Guide for Baking and Diet

By SmartKitchen Picks India · Last updated 2026-10-27

A digital kitchen scale is the easiest way to make baking consistent and to track portions for diet or fitness. Measuring by weight is more accurate than cups.

Here are the features that matter and a quick test to check one at home.

Features to check

  • Accuracy: look for 1 gram steps, or finer if you weigh spices or coffee.
  • Capacity: 5 kg is enough for most home use.
  • Tare function: lets you reset to zero with a bowl on top.
  • Unit switching: grams, ounces and millilitres are useful for recipes from different sources.
  • Platform size: it should hold a mixing bowl without covering the display.
  • Battery: standard AAA or a coin cell that is easy to replace, plus an auto-off.

A simple test at home

  • Weigh something with a known weight, such as a sealed packet of sugar printed with its net weight.
  • Put the same item on three times. The reading should be the same each time.
  • Check that the scale returns to zero when empty.

Using it well

  • Place it on a flat, hard surface.
  • Do not exceed the capacity.
  • Wipe with a damp cloth and never submerge it.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate should a kitchen scale be?

For general cooking, 1 gram accuracy is enough. Choose 0.1 gram models only if you weigh very small amounts.

Why does my scale show different weights each time?

Low batteries, an uneven surface or a faulty sensor are the usual causes. Replace the battery and try again.

Do I need a scale for Indian cooking?

It is not essential for everyday dal and sabzi, but it is very useful for baking, protein tracking and portion control.
