How to Choose a Masala Box (Masala Dabba) for Daily Cooking

By SmartKitchen Picks India · Last updated 2026-10-27

A masala dabba keeps your most-used spices within reach, which makes cooking faster. The best ones also keep spices fresh and are easy to clean.

Here is what separates a good one from a frustrating one.

Material

  • Stainless steel is durable, does not stain from turmeric and lasts for years.
  • Plastic is lighter and cheaper but can stain and absorb strong smells.
  • Check the listing for the steel grade or a food-grade plastic rating.

Lid design

  • A tight outer lid keeps out moisture and air.
  • A clear or inner lid lets you see the spices and reduces how often you open the main lid.
  • Test that the lid fits firmly with no wobble.

Layout and size

  • Seven compartments suit most Indian cooking: for example turmeric, red chilli, coriander, cumin, mustard, garam masala and salt.
  • Check that the compartments are big enough for a tablespoon.
  • A small spoon included is useful.

Keeping spices fresh

Fill the box only with what you will use in a few weeks and keep the rest of the stock in airtight jars. Keep it away from steam and direct heat near the stove, and wash and dry it completely before refilling.

Frequently asked questions

Stainless steel or plastic masala box?

Steel lasts longer and resists staining. Plastic is lighter and cheaper but wears out sooner.

How many compartments do I need?

Seven covers most daily cooking. More compartments may be useful if you use many whole spices.

How do I remove turmeric stains from a plastic box?

Wash with warm soapy water and leave it in sunlight for a few hours. It may not fully remove old stains.
